# Shopping lists (LST)

Creating, viewing, archiving, duplicating and resetting shopping lists. Part of the [functional requirements](functional-requirements.md), which explain how requirements and their criteria are written and show the status of every requirement.

## LST-1

🎯 [MVP] Create, rename and delete a list. Deleting requires confirmation.

## LST-2

🎯 [MVP] View all active lists. View archived lists separately.

## LST-3

🎯 [MVP] Archive and unarchive a list. An archived list is read-only until unarchived.

## LST-4

🎯 [MVP] Duplicate a list. The items are copied and all start unchecked. Shares are not copied.

## LST-5

🎯 [MVP] "Uncheck all" resets every item on the list.

## LST-6

🎯 [MVP] "Save as template" creates a template from the list's items.
