# ADR 0066 — Managing lists phase details

- **Status:** ✅ Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Context

[PH-16c](../roadmap.md#ph-16c-managing-lists) lets a guest delete lists and shows a page for an address that leads nowhere. Since [PH-16b](../roadmap.md#ph-16b-workspace-from-the-default-data) it had also gathered the fixes of the lists screens and of the SPA's writes: the new list's Create button ([OP-115](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/318)), the session check before each write ([OP-116](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/319)), one anonymous session across tabs ([OP-120](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/329)), the handling of a weak connection ([OP-040](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/99)), a change sent twice ([#320](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/320)), the raccoon off the window's edge ([#323](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/323)), two findings of the [security audit of 2026-11-01](../audits/2026-11-01-security-audit.md) ([#345](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/345), [#346](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/346)) and the app's version in the menu ([OP-130](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/380)). About eleven items and at least two new concepts are more than a small phase holds ([ADR 0014](0014-roadmap.md), decision 6).

The [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-PH-16c-managing-lists-design.md) holds the design in full; this ADR records the decisions and the alternatives. All were settled in the brainstorm.

## Changes to the roadmap

- **PH-16c is split** (decision 1). PH-16c Managing lists keeps deleting, the not-found pages and the lists screens' fixes; the new [PH-16d Resilient changes](../roadmap.md#ph-16d-resilient-changes) ([#382](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/382)) takes OP-040's part, #320, OP-116 and OP-120, with [ACC-1](../requirements/ACC-accounts-and-guest-mode.md#acc-1).6.
- **PH-16c gains** [LST-1](../requirements/LST-shopping-lists.md#lst-1).6 to LST-1.9, [APP-5](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-5).2, [OP-130](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/380), [#345](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/345) and [#346](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/346).

## Decisions

| # | Topic | Options considered | Decision and reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The phase's size | Keeping everything in one phase. | **Split in two, in this order: PH-16c Managing lists (deleting with its confirmation and selecting lists, the not-found pages, the lists screens' fixes), then PH-16d Resilient changes (timeouts, the "not saved" state, refetching after a failure, a change sent once, no session check before each write, one anonymous session across tabs)** _(the owner's choice, on Claude's proposal)_. Each half brings one concept. The handling of a weak connection lives in the shared client code, so done second it covers creating, renaming and deleting at once, with no rework; both come before [PH-17a](../roadmap.md#ph-17a-list-items) adds the many writes of items. OP-116 and OP-120 change the same `withSession`, next to the timeouts, so they stay together. |
| 2 | Deleting from the list's screen | A Delete button on the screen. | **Delete in the list's ⋮ menu, next to Rename; after the confirmation, the lists page opens** _(the owner's choice)_. The menu already holds the list's actions ([LST-1](../requirements/LST-shopping-lists.md#lst-1).2). |
| 3 | Deleting from the lists page | Swipe to delete; checkboxes always shown on a desktop; a long press as the only way in. | **A selection mode: "Select" in the header starts it on every device, and on a phone a long press on a list starts it with that list selected. While selecting, a tap toggles a list instead of opening it; the header becomes a selection bar with ✕, the count, "Select all" or "Deselect all", and Delete, disabled with nothing selected; on a phone "New list" is hidden. ✕, Esc and Back stop selecting. Selecting started by a long press stops when the last list is deselected; started by "Select", it stays at zero. After deleting, the mode stops and the lists page stays** _(the owner's choices; the "Select" button on Claude's recommendation)_. A long press alone cannot be discovered, and checkboxes always shown would make the desktop work otherwise than the phone; one button works the same everywhere, and the long press stays as a phone's shortcut. The bar has room for Archive later ([LST-2](../requirements/LST-shopping-lists.md#lst-2).2). |
| 4 | The confirmation | A toast with undo instead of asking ([APP-3](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-3).2); listing the selected lists' names. | **A modal dialog, shadcn/ui's `AlertDialog` on Base UI: "Delete list "X"?" or "Delete N lists?", "This can't be undone.", Cancel with the focus and a red Delete; Esc cancels, and a click outside does nothing. When the delete fails, the dialog stays open and says so; on success no message is shown until toasts come with [APP-3](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-3).1 in PH-17a** _(the owner's choices on Claude's proposal; the component Claude's call; at the plan, the owner kept Base UI's alert dialog, which a click outside does not close)_. Deleting cannot be undone, so [APP-3](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-3).3 asks first; the count is enough to confirm. `AlertDialog` is the family of the `Sheet` and `DropdownMenu` already in the app and traps the focus as a modal must. |
| 5 | The delete API | A `DELETE` per selected list; a soft delete. | **`DELETE /api/lists/:id` for one list, and `POST /api/lists/delete` with `{ ids }` for several, deleting them in one transaction: all or none. A list is removed from the database, not marked** _(the owner's choice of one request; the rest Claude's call)_. Many requests could leave some lists deleted and some not on a failure. Archiving is a separate, reversible feature later. |
| 6 | Not-found pages | One page for every address; a joke in the text; the raccoon. | **A path that is not a page shows "Nothing here" and "This page doesn't exist."; a list's address that is not a list ID, or names a list the user cannot open, shows "Nothing here" and "This list doesn't exist or was deleted." Both have "Back to lists" and, in the middle, a grey old-style metal trash can lying empty with its open end toward the viewer, its lid leaning beside it, in the raccoon's style and colours. An ID that is not a UUID is answered by the SPA and never reaches the API** _(the owner's choices; the ID check Claude's call)_. The words stay factual. Checking the ID in the SPA closes [#345](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/345): no address can send the SPA's requests to another API path. A foreign list looks like a missing one, as in the API ([NFR-4](../requirements/NFR-non-functional-requirements.md#nfr-4).10). |
| 7 | The new list's screen | Today's: the name turns into the title when the field is left. | **The name stays a bordered field until the list is created, with "Create", which creates it as Enter does. On a phone "Create" is pinned at the bottom, where "New list" was; on a desktop it stands after the field. It is disabled while the name is empty or too long, and while the create is in flight** _(the owner's direction in [OP-115](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/318); the placement and the states on Claude's proposal)_. Nothing on the screen suggests a list that does not exist. |
| 8 | Characters in a list's name | Refusing only NUL. | **`listNameSchema` refuses Unicode control and format characters (categories `Cc` and `Cf`), with a 400** _(Claude's call)_. Bidirectional controls can make a name read otherwise than it is once [SHR](../requirements/SHR-sharing.md) shows names to others, and a NUL answered 500 ([#346](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/346)). |
| 9 | The version in the menu | The release number alone; the version baked into the SPA. | **A line `Version 0.2.0 · a1b2c3d` above "Schema version" in the menu, both values from `/api/health`, which gains the release version** _(the owner's choice: both the release and the exact build)_. A demo built from a branch carries the last release's number; the commit tells it apart. The health answer already has the commit in both its variants, so the line shows even when the database is down. |
| 10 | The raccoon on a desktop | Dropping the page's scrollbar gutter. | **The empty list's raccoon reaches the window's right edge while the page keeps `scrollbar-gutter: stable`** _(Claude's call)_. The gutter stops the layout from jumping between short and long pages ([#323](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/323)). |

## Settled while building

- **The name check allows the zero-width joiner (U+200D) and the tag characters (U+E0020 to U+E007F)**, though they are in category `Cf`: family and flag emoji (`👨‍👩‍👧`, `🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿`) are built from them, so refusing them would break names the app should accept. The other `Cc` and `Cf` characters stay refused.
- **The release version reaches the image by copying the root `package.json`, not by a build argument.** The API reads `../../../package.json` relative to its source, which is the repository's root file both in the tree and in the image once its final stage has `COPY package.json ./`. The version is then in the copied files already, release-please's bump of that file is the only place to change it, and no builder (Compose, CI, `demo-up`) has to pass it, as they do for `GIT_COMMIT`.
- **`alert-dialog.tsx` is restyled from the generated file**: Szop's card and surface tokens replace the popover tokens it does not define (which failed the axe contrast check), and the imports end in `.ts`. A later `shadcn add` must not overwrite it blindly.
- **The selection mode is held in the history entry's state** (`navigate("/", { state: { selecting: true } })`, read with `isSelecting(useLocation().state)`), not in component state: Back then stops it without leaving the page, and the shell can read it to drop its header. Every other way out runs `navigate(-1)`, so no stray entries stay. The target is `"/"`, not `"."`: `"."` from an index route adds `?index` to the address. Alternative considered: component state plus a `popstate` listener, which would have left Back to leave the page. Shortcut: a reload on the selecting entry keeps the state (the browser keeps it), and Cancel selection then goes back one entry, which may be outside the app.
- **A long press is detected by pointer events on the row, not by the `contextmenu` event**, and works only on `(pointer: coarse)`. The click that ends it is swallowed in the capture phase (`preventDefault` and `stopPropagation`), so it neither opens the list nor toggles the checkbox that replaced the link.

## Consequences

- The SPA gains its first modal dialog and its first selection mode; archiving reuses the bar.
- Deleted lists are gone from the database; their items, once they exist, go with them.
- PH-16c leaves every write as fragile on a weak connection as PH-16a made it; PH-16d follows it.
