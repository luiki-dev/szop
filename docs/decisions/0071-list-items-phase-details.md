# ADR 0071 — List items phase details

- **Status:** ✅ Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-06

## Context

[PH-17](../roadmap.md#ph-17-list-items) brings items to a list: adding them by name, editing every field, checking them into the haul and back, removing them with an undo, and sorting the list by category or in the order added. PH-17a (items) and PH-17b (the haul) were merged back into one phase as an experiment with a larger phase ([context-size audit](../audits/2026-11-06-ph-17-context-experiment.md)).

The owner described how they shop: items are added one after another; an item is edited rarely, the quantity most often, then the note and the price; items are checked off while being put into the basket, with one hand on the cart and only the right thumb free.

The [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-PH-17-list-items-design.md) holds the design in full, with the approved mockups; this ADR records the decisions and the alternatives. All were settled in the brainstorm.

## Changes to the roadmap

- **PH-17 also delivers** [ITM-2](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-2) (part: ITM-2.3 and the new ITM-2.4, adding a typed name) and the new requirement [APR-2](../requirements/APR-appearance.md#apr-2) (the checkboxes' side).
- **[PH-21](../roadmap.md#ph-21-shop-in-a-store) gains** [ITM-1](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-1).3 (the list owner's currency) and the amount parts of [ORD-1](../requirements/ORD-list-display-and-ordering.md#ord-1).5 and ORD-1.8 (the haul's sums); PH-17 shows prices with a fixed €.
- **[PH-23](../roadmap.md#ph-23-smart-input) gains** the new [ITM-2](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-2).5, entering a quantity right after picking a suggestion.
- **[ORD-5](../requirements/ORD-list-display-and-ordering.md#ord-5).2's second part** ("items added together in one action keep that action's order") ➡️ [ITM-5](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-5).3, delivered with the catalog browser's batch add ([PH-24](../roadmap.md#ph-24-catalog-browser)).
- **Bug [#397](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/397)** (a blink behind ✕ when Select starts on the lists page) is fixed in PH-17.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Options considered | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The checkbox's side | Left only, as in the approved list design ([ADR 0022](0022-ring-tail-redesign.md)); right only; swiping a row to check it. | **A setting, Left or Right, with Right the default. With Right, the name and note sit on the left and the quantity and price before the checkbox at the right edge; with Left, today's layout. It applies to every list and to the haul drawer's ↶ buttons.** The owner shops with the right thumb; a checkbox on the left is out of its reach. Swiping is a 💡 future extension: it checks items by accident while scrolling, clashes with iOS's edge swipe back, is hard to discover, and needs the checkbox anyway for accessibility. The owner chose the setting and Right as the default. |
| 2 | Where the checkbox setting lives | A settings screen; the list's ⋮ menu (per list). | **In the menu's footer, under Color scheme, as the same two-option radio control named "Checkboxes" with a small picture of a row; kept on the device like the color scheme.** Claude's proposal; the owner accepted it for now, since the settings will soon need a menu or screen of their own ([OP-133](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/398)). |
| 3 | Opening an item's edit | A long press (tiring when done often); an edit button on each row. | **Tapping the item's name opens its edit; the checkbox only checks.** The owner chose it. |
| 4 | The edit's form | A page of its own; editing in the row. | **A bottom sheet on a phone (the list dimmed behind it) and a centered dialog on a desktop, titled "Edit item". The fields in order: Name; Quantity with − and + and the Unit in one row; Price per unit with the line total under it; Category; Note last, since it can grow. Buttons: Remove (on the left), Cancel and Save. No field takes the focus when it opens.** The owner set the order by how often each field is edited and moved the note last so a long note does not push the category out of view. |
| 5 | Picking a category | A select with the flat list; a tree inside the edit sheet. | **A second sheet over the edit sheet (the dialog's content on a desktop) with "Search categories" and the tree. The rows sit in one bordered box with thin lines, as on the Catalog page, and end with a radio circle. The chevron opens and closes a category's children; the rest of the row picks it and goes back. Every level below the top sits on one muted band and is indented 20 px per level. "No category" comes first, in italics.** The owner asked for rows that read as choices, and for one band rather than a darker one per level. |
| 6 | Removing an item | Asking "Are you sure?"; removing without undo. | **Remove closes the sheet and the item leaves the list at once; a toast "Removed *Bananas*" with Undo shows above the add bar for about 5 seconds, and stays while it has the focus or the pointer. Undo creates the item again with the same ID and every field, so it returns to its place.** [APP-3](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-3).2. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 7 | Undoing a check made by mistake | A toast after every check. | **The haul's first chip is the undo: a tap puts the item back.** A toast after every check would cover the list while shopping. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 8 | Adding an item | Keeping the typed name in the field. | **Enter or "Add" adds the typed name as an item with no catalog product and no category; the field clears and keeps the focus, so the next item can be typed at once.** The owner asked for it. Entering a quantity right after picking a catalog suggestion is the owner's wish too, but suggestions are [PH-23](../roadmap.md#ph-23-smart-input)'s: it becomes [ITM-2](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-2).5 there. |
| 9 | The sort setting's place | A switch in the list's header; the settings. | **The first item of the list's ⋮ menu, "Sort by category", a check item, then a line, then Rename and Delete. A tap switches it, closes the menu, and the list re-orders at once.** Claude's proposal; the owner approved the mockup. |
| 10 | The list with sorting off | Category cards in the order added. | **One card with no category name, its header saying only "N left", the items oldest first, so a new item appears at the bottom next to the add bar.** The owner chose oldest first. With sorting on, items with no category are in a last card named "No category", in italics (the owner's choice). |
| 11 | The sort setting's storage | In the browser, per device. | **A column of the list, `lists.sort_by_category`, `true` by default; saved on the server, so everyone on the list sees the same order.** [ORD-5](../requirements/ORD-list-display-and-ordering.md#ord-5) makes it the list's. It is the first migration that changes an existing table, so it brings the migration test with data ([OP-052](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/93)). Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 12 | The haul in PH-17 | All of the approved haul at once, sums included. | **The haul as designed in [ADR 0022](0022-ring-tail-redesign.md) without its three sums and without "Put all back": the header shows "The haul" and the count, the bar follows the count. Chips show the name and the line total (the name alone without a price); the drawer's rows show the name, quantity and unit, line total and ↶.** The sums ([ITM-8](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-8)) and "Put all back" ([LST-5](../requirements/LST-shopping-lists.md#lst-5)) are PH-21's, where the roadmap puts them; a line total is the item's own price, shown already in the list's rows. The owner first chose chips with names only, then agreed to line totals after the haul's review. |
| 13 | The currency until PH-21 | The list owner's currency now ([ITM-1](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-1).3). | **A fixed € everywhere; the owner's currency comes with the sums in PH-21.** Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 14 | The table of items | Items inside the list as JSON. | **A table `list_items` with the key `(workspace_id, id)` and an ID made in the browser (UUIDv7), like lists. Its list, category and unit are foreign keys that include `workspace_id`; deleting the list deletes its items, deleting a category or unit sets only that column to null. A name of 1 to 100 characters and a note of at most 500 are checked by the database, the API and the form ([LIM-2](../requirements/LIM-limits.md#lim-2)). A reference to another workspace's record gets the same 404 as one that does not exist ([NFR-4](../requirements/NFR-non-functional-requirements.md#nfr-4).10).** Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 15 | The checked state ([OP-082](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/106), [OP-040](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/99)) | A `checked` boolean next to the time; keeping the first check's time. | **One column, `checked_at`; an item is checked when it is set. The API takes the state wanted, `checked: true` or `false`, never a toggle. Every `checked: true` sets the current time, the time of the latest check; `false` clears it. The haul is ordered by `checked_at`, newest first.** The owner chose the latest check's time: the haul needs it, and later features may. |
| 16 | The order added ([OP-041](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/100), position part) | A `position` column. | **No column: the order added is the order of the IDs, which UUIDv7 makes time-ordered.** Moving items by hand is a 💡 future extension; a batch add (PH-24) can make increasing IDs. A position comes when a feature needs it. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 17 | Quantities and money ([OP-041](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/100), money part) | Decimal numbers; floating point. | **A quantity in whole thousandths and a price per unit in whole cents, both integers; the line total is `round(price × quantity / 1000)`, computed once per line by one function in `packages/shared`. Fields accept a decimal comma and a decimal point.** No floating-point error, one rounding rule for the list, the haul and later the sums. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 18 | The drawer and the toast ([OP-081](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/105)) | `vaul` for the drawer; `sonner` for toasts. | **Base UI's Drawer for the haul's drawer and the edit sheet, its Toast for the undo, and its NumberField for − and +; all in the installed `@base-ui/react` 1.9, which sets styles through classes and CSS variables and injects no `<style>` element, so the content security policy needs no change.** One library already in the project. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 19 | Forms | Controlled inputs by hand, as the list name today. | **React Hook Form, with the zod schemas of `packages/shared` as its resolver, for the edit form and the add bar.** The roadmap names it for this phase. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 20 | Changes show at once | Waiting for the server, as lists do today. | **Adding, editing, checking, unchecking and removing an item update the screen at once (optimistic updates), on top of PH-16d's time limit, "not saved" state and reload after a failure ([ADR 0070](0070-resilient-changes-phase-details.md)).** [APP-7](../requirements/APP-app-wide-behavior.md#app-7).1. |
| 21 | A guest's first change and default data ([OP-048](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/101)) | A special path for the first change. | **Nothing special: a guest's first change in PH-17 is always creating a list, and the workspace's copy of the default data keeps its IDs, so an item with a default category or unit is saved as any other. One test proves it.** [ACC-1](../requirements/ACC-accounts-and-guest-mode.md#acc-1).5. Claude's proposal; the owner agreed. |
| 22 | The acceptance criteria | Keeping them as drafted. | **Reviewed with the owner:** new ITM-2.4 (the field clears, the focus stays), ITM-2.5 (PH-23), ITM-6.3 (tapping the name opens the edit), ITM-7.4 (the latest check's time), ORD-5.5 (sorting off), and APR-2; ORD-2.4 names the "No category" card; ORD-5.1 says where the setting is; APP-3.2 names the toast; ACC-1.5 no longer names a catalog product. ITM-1, ITM-6, ITM-7, ORD-2, ORD-3, ORD-5, APP-6 and APP-7 are finalized. The owner accepted every change. |
| 23 | A toast's width | The full width of the list's cards. | **75 % of the screen's width, centered, for every toast.** The owner asked for it: a toast as wide as the cards reads as part of the list. |
| 24 | A text over its limit ([LIM-2](../requirements/LIM-limits.md#lim-2).2) | "At most 100 characters." under the field, as the list name shows since PH-16a. | **Every text field with a limit takes no character past it (`maxlength`), with no message: the add bar, the item's name and note, and the list name's field, which changes in PH-17. The API and the database keep their checks.** The owner chose it: a message under the pinned add bar moves the bar, and it would be the bar's only error; a longer paste is cut without a word, which names of shopping items rarely meet. |
| 25 | The add bar over the scrolling list | The bar floating over the cards; the last 28 px of the list fading into the page color. | **The add bar on its own band in the page color, with rounded top corners like the haul, a soft shadow and a thin top edge, reaching under the haul; the cards slide under it.** The owner chose it on a mockup: the cards were cut at a straight line above the bar, floating reads as unclear, and a fade is barely seen on dark cards. |

## Consequences

- Every later item feature (suggestions, the catalog browser, sharing) builds on `list_items`, the integer quantity and money, and the optimistic updates.
- The settings now hold two choices in the menu's footer; a third calls for [OP-133](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/398).
- PH-21 adds the sums, the currency and "Put all back" to a haul that already works.
- A migration that changes an existing table is tested with data from now on.

## Settled while building

- ✏️ **The line total is a JS number, not a 32-bit integer.** At the maxima (price 9 999 999 cents, quantity 9 999 000 thousandths) the line total is 99 989 990 001, past 32 bits. It is computed as a JavaScript number, exact below 2^53, and never stored. This corrects the spec's note that a line total stays within a 32-bit integer.
- ✏️ **`list_items` also has `list_items_workspace_id_fkey` to `workspaces`.** The spec's constraint list leaves it out, but the schema test that every workspace-owned table references `workspaces` directly (and `lists` does) requires it; it cascades like theirs.
- ✏️ **`migratedDatabase` empties its database with `resetDatabase`, not `DROP DATABASE`.** The plan said to drop and create it; `DROP DATABASE` waits for a checkpoint and outlasts the hook timeout under load ([#317](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/317)), which the testing guide already avoids.
- ✏️ **The API shape.** `GET /api/lists/:id` returns the list with its items (`listWithItemsSchema`), in the order added (the IDs' order, since they are time-ordered). `sortByCategory` is a field of every list, in the lists' list too. `PATCH /api/lists/:id` takes `updateListSchema`: the name, `sortByCategory`, or both, at least one; it replaces the rename-only body, and `renameListSchema` is gone.
- ✏️ **A foreign-key violation is the 404.** The item routes do not look a category or unit up before writing. The composite foreign keys refuse an ID of another workspace, PostgreSQL answers error `23503`, and the repository turns it (found on the error or its `cause`, where Drizzle wraps it) into `null`, which the route answers as the one 404. A list deleted between the access check and the write ends the same way. A create whose ID is taken by an item of another list of the same workspace is the same 404.
- ✏️ **Checking is a field of the update.** `PATCH /api/lists/:listId/items/:id` takes `checked: true | false` beside the editable fields, not a route of its own. `true` stores the time of the call every time ([ITM-7.4](../requirements/ITM-list-items.md#itm-7)); `false` clears it. Every write answers 401 without a session, as the delete routes of the lists do.
- ✏️ **The sort setting's failure toast has its own words.** The setting has no item name, so the toast says "Sorting wasn't saved. Check your connection and try again."
- ✏️ **The category picker is a radio group of `role="radio"` buttons, not native radios.** A native radio group answers an arrow key by picking, which would close the picker on the first arrow press; buttons pick on Enter, Space or a click only. On a phone the picker is a second Drawer rendered while it is open (no exit animation); on a desktop it replaces the dialog's content and the form stays mounted, hidden, so its values are kept.
- ✏️ **Each E2E test is its own client address.** The anonymous sign-in limit (30 an hour per address) was drawn by every test of every project from one bucket, because Better Auth could not resolve a client address, and the last tests of a full run were refused (F26). The E2E server (`e2e/start-server.ts`) sets `TRUSTED_PROXIES=127.0.0.1`, and the `context` fixture of `e2e/support/test.ts` sends a random `X-Forwarded-For` per test. The production limit and its code are unchanged.
- ✏️ **The list is refetched when its last pending change settles.** Not after each failure: a refetch while other changes are in flight would answer without them and overwrite their optimistic state. A burst of changes costs one GET, whether the changes succeed or fail.
- ✏️ **The add bar is sticky, not fixed.** It sticks to the bottom of the content column (`sticky bottom-0` inside the page's column), so it stays within the 720 px column beside the desktop sidebar; "Add" does not take the focus from the field (`mousedown` is not default-handled), so the phone's keyboard stays open. The toast region's `bottom-20` clears the bar (73 px high); Task 8's haul raises it.
- ✏️ **A failed catalog read shows the list's error card.** The items need the catalog's categories and units, so "Try again" retries both reads.
- ✏️ **One message for every bad number.** A quantity or a price that `parseAmount` refuses, or that is outside the limits, shows "Enter a number, such as 1.5." ("1." is refused too, with that message; the spec asks for no more). No new copy.
- ✏️ **The quantity is a text input with its own − and +, not NumberField.** NumberField parses by the locale, which would refuse the decimal comma the spec accepts ("0,5"); the form keeps the text and `parseAmount` reads it. − is disabled at 1 or below or on an empty or invalid field; + on an empty field gives 1.
- ✏️ **A note is saved as typed.** The spec asks for no trimming of notes, so a whitespace-only note stays a note; only an empty one becomes `null`.
- ✏️ **`EditItem` also takes `listId`,** which its item hooks need. Until Task 7 the Category field shows the name and opens nothing.
- ✏️ **The add bar and the haul are one sticky block.** The list page wraps them in one `sticky bottom-0` container (`mt-auto`), so the bar sits above the haul and both stay at the bottom of short and long lists and within the 720 px column. The add bar's own `sticky` is gone. The toast region's offset is `bottom-48`, clearing the bar and the collapsed haul.
- ✏️ **The haul measures its chips from hidden copies.** A `ResizeObserver` and a hidden, `aria-hidden` row of the same chips give their widths to `fitChips`; without layout (width 0) every chip is shown. The drawer opens by a drag up on the header, detected with a window `pointerup`. The move into the haul is a 180 ms chip entrance under `motion-safe:`; the row leaves its card at once.
