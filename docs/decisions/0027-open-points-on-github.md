# ADR 0027 — Open points on GitHub, plans in the roadmap

- **Status:** ✅ Accepted — decisions 1 and 4 refined by [ADR 0047](0047-drop-the-readme-roadmap-diagram.md) (the README's diagram is dropped)
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Completes:** [ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md), decision 2 (the trial ends, see decisions 1 to 7)
- **Supersedes:** [ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md), decision 14 (the trial rule, see decisions 2 to 4); [ADR 0011](0011-design-sanity-check-follow-ups.md), decision 1 (the central register, see decisions 2, 5 and 6)
- **Refines:** [ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md), decisions 1 (GitHub the source of status and open points, `roadmap.md` of a phase's plan, see decision 3) and 11 (the issue's body is a copy, see decision 3); [ADR 0011](0011-design-sanity-check-follow-ups.md), decision 4 (the document roles, see Consequences); [ADR 0014](0014-roadmap.md), decision 4 and [ADR 0017](0017-readme-roadmap-diagram.md), decision 4 (the trial's status rule made permanent, see decision 4)
- **Revisits:** [ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md), decision 3 (closed open points become issues too, see decision 5)

## Context

[ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md) moved Szop's tracking to GitHub Issues and Projects as a trial: for two phases, PH-06b and PH-07, GitHub was the source of truth and the docs (`roadmap.md`, `open-points.md`, the README's diagram) were its mirror, brought in line before every PR. [OP-086](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/156) was to decide how the trial ends.

The trial worked, and it showed what to keep. The [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-open-points-on-github-design.md) holds the evidence ([Evidence from the trial](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-open-points-on-github-design.md#evidence-from-the-trial)); in short:

- **No drift.** On 2026-10-11 all 60 open open-point issues matched their register entries in phase, status and issue number.
- **A double cost.** Every open-point change was made twice; PH-07 alone made about 20 issue edits, each mirrored by hand.
- **One mistake, caught by GitHub.** Claude misread the register's grouping; GitHub refused the change that would have copied the error.
- **What the owner read:** GitHub for status and what comes next, `roadmap.md` for a phase's scope, the README's diagram. Nobody read `open-points.md`.
- **What dropping the register costs:** 282 mentions of `open-points.md` in living docs, among them 217 links to an entry, and 241 link targets in specs, plans and the audit.

The design was brainstormed with the owner and written as a [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-open-points-on-github-design.md). This ADR records its decisions. The day-to-day rules are in [project tracking](../development/project-tracking.md#keeping-github-and-the-docs-in-step).

## Decisions

| # | Topic | Options considered | Decision and reasoning |
|---|-------|--------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Which side the owner reads | — | **Both, for different things:** GitHub for status and what comes next, `roadmap.md` for a phase's scope. The README's diagram stays. The decisions below follow from this. <br>✏️ **Refined by [ADR 0047](0047-drop-the-readme-roadmap-diagram.md), decision 1:** the README's diagram is dropped. |
| 2 | `open-points.md` | Drop it, the issues being the only place; keep it as the mirror; keep a slim index | **Drop it: open points live only on GitHub.** The links to its entries in living docs, ADRs included, are rewritten to the issues once, by script. Keeping the mirror keeps the double bookkeeping for nothing anyone reads; a slim index still needs an edit for every add, move or close. What is lost: reading open points offline or without GitHub. <br>**Settled while building:** the script rewrote 257 links in 35 living docs (entries to their issues, phase groups to the phase issues, the three ADR links to the Open Points view), and an independent check matched every rewritten link with its target's title. The few links to the register without an anchor were reworded by hand in the guides. |
| 3 | Where a phase's plan lives | `roadmap.md` is the source; the issue stays the source and `roadmap.md` its mirror; the issue is the only place | **`roadmap.md` is the source of each phase's plan; the phase issue keeps its body as a copy** synced from it, the owner's choice: the issue reads on its own on the board without a jump to the roadmap. The duplication is accepted; the direction is fixed (docs to issue). The issue keeps what only GitHub does well: sub-issues, blocking links, status and fields. A scope change is a docs change again, reviewed in the PR's diff. The phase issues' bodies are not changed by this ADR: they already hold the plan. |
| 4 | Phase status in `roadmap.md` | Keep the stage tables with their Status column; drop the column; drop the tables | **Keep the tables as they are, Status column included,** mirroring GitHub. It changes at the same three moments as the diagram (work starts, the PR opens, the merge) and in the same commit, so it costs almost nothing and keeps `roadmap.md` readable on its own. <br>✏️ **Refined by [ADR 0047](0047-drop-the-readme-roadmap-diagram.md), decision 2:** the diagram is dropped; the stage tables mirror the status alone. |
| 5 | The register's closed entries | Freeze them in an archive document; make each a closed issue; let git history keep them | **Each becomes a closed issue,** the owner's choice: every open point then lives on GitHub, closed or open, and every link in a living doc goes to an issue. 25 new issues; OP-012, OP-057 and OP-067 already have one. <br>**Settled while building:** the 25 are issues [#167](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/167) to [#191](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/191), each closed as completed with its "settled by" line as the comment. Each is a sub-issue of what settled it: the phase issue when a PR did (through the PR column of `roadmap.md`), the `adr` issue when an ADR did. A reference to another open point inside a closed entry reads `OP-NNN (#N)` (one case, OP-022's). One entry wrote its list as `- **Parts:**`, which the generator missed, so the issue of OP-062 ([#189](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/189)) got its ticked Parts checklist by hand. |
| 6 | What happens to the file | A stub with a frozen index, at the same path; move it whole to an archive folder | **Moved whole to `docs/archive/open-points.md`,** the owner's proposal, with a `CLAUDE.md` in the folder saying nothing there is a source. The records (specs, plans, the audit) keep their anchors, re-pointed to the archive by a path substitution, so each still shows the register as it was. A stub would break those anchors. <br>**Settled while building:** 190 links in the records (138 in specs and plans, 52 in the audit) were re-pointed. Some plans had `../open-points.md` links inside draft text meant for files in other folders; a later pass repaired the records' broken links where they resolved to one file (354 links, 44 of them to the register), and left the 12 that resolve to none. |
| 7 | Where ADRs link | To the archive, like the records; to GitHub, like the living docs | **To GitHub,** the owner's choice: ADRs are read for current state, so their links go to the issues, and their three links to "the open points register" to the board's [Open Points view](https://github.com/users/luiki-dev/projects/3/views/5). Their wording stays. |

## Consequences

- **Every open point is an issue.** The 25 closed entries are now closed issues, so 88 issues are labelled `open-point` (OP-001 to OP-088), 29 of them closed when this ADR is merged (OP-086 included).
- **`docs/open-points.md` no longer exists.** The register is in `docs/archive/open-points.md`, frozen on 2026-10-11 under a banner, with OP-067 and OP-086 moved to Closed. `docs/archive/CLAUDE.md` tells Claude that nothing in the folder is a source and how to read open points instead (`gh issue list --label open-point`, the board).
- **Links follow the rule of decisions 2, 6 and 7.** The living docs link the issues, the phase issues and the Open Points view; the specs, plans and the audit link the archive, anchors unchanged. A new open point needs no edit in any document.
- **[Project tracking](../development/project-tracking.md#keeping-github-and-the-docs-in-step) holds the permanent rules:** who owns which fact, when each copy changes, how an open point is added, moved and closed, and that the PR description lists the issue changes it made. The trial rule is gone from it, from `CLAUDE.md`, from the [definition of done](../development/definition-of-done.md) (items 6 and 9), from the PR template, from the open-point and phase issue forms, and from [`phase-walkthrough.md`](../development/phase-walkthrough.md).
- **The document roles of [ADR 0011](0011-design-sanity-check-follow-ups.md), decision 4, change:** the register's row is replaced by the GitHub issues (the source of open points, status and what comes next), and `docs/archive/` is added as frozen records that are never a source. `roadmap.md` is the source of each phase's plan.
- **The board's views are documented as they are set,** six of them (Timeline, Backlog, Board, Open Points, Next Up and Owner Steps), with their filters, grouping and fields, read from the API. The project has views 1 and 3 to 7.
- **What is lost:** reading the open points offline, or without GitHub, and the register's single page to scan. `gh` and the board's Open Points view replace them, and git history keeps the archive.
- **Not changed:** the phase issues' bodies, the README's diagram, and the rule against syncing by automation (a rule kept from [ADR 0024](0024-github-project-tracking.md), decision 14). Accepted ADRs, specs, plans and the audit keep their wording; only their links changed.
- **[OP-086](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/156) and this ADR's [issue](https://github.com/luiki-dev/szop/issues/166) are closed by the PR that merges it.**
