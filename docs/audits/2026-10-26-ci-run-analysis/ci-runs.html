Szop CI runs

How long Szop's GitHub Actions runs, jobs and steps take, and how often they run. Durations are measured on successful runs, from the first job's start to the last job's end. Reruns are left out of durations unless included below: a rerun keeps the passed jobs of its first attempt, so its span covers both attempts. The analysis and its findings are in the audit report.

Installing Playwright's browsers

The e2e job's step "Install Playwright's browsers" in every CI run, whatever its result. It installs system packages with apt, and a slow Ubuntu mirror makes it the source of the longest runs. The dashed line is 2 minutes.
